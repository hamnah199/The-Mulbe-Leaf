Welcome to The Mulbé Leaf, part of The Mulbé Leaf. This is a calm space for everyday skincare and beauty guidance. We write simple, unfussy guides on routines, ingredients, haircare and makeup, so you can explore what feels right for you without pressure or hype.

Our articles are general information, not medical advice. For specific or persistent concerns, we always encourage speaking with a qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What will I find here?

Gentle routines, ingredient explainers, sun protection tips, haircare basics and approachable makeup guides, all written in plain language.

Are you giving medical advice?

No. Everything here is general information to help you explore. If something is persistent, painful or worrying, please see a dermatologist or doctor.

Do I need lots of products?

Not at all. We believe in fewer products and clear steps, so a routine is easy to keep. Every step can be adapted, swapped or skipped to suit you.

Can I suggest a topic?

Please do. Send us a note on the contact page with any question or article idea.
